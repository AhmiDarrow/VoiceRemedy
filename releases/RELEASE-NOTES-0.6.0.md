# VoiceRemedy 0.6.0

Added four public-domain teaching selections:
- Row, Row, Row Your Boat
- Are You Sleeping? / Frere Jacques
- London Bridge Is Falling Down
- Ode to Joy (opening theme sung on doo; no modern lyric translation)

The songbook now has six songs/themes, 34 song lessons, and 31 bundled neural narration clips. Short lines lead into connected verses/themes; difficult whole-song attempts return to the weakest measured line. Quicker notes use a slower teaching tempo so there is time to listen, respond, and obtain enough pitch samples.

Added optional live-pitch-hint suppression while retaining end-of-round feedback, practical rest/comfort guidance, and clearer explanation of what pitch scores do and do not measure.

Research rationale and sources: TEACHING-EVIDENCE.md. Evidence supports practice and can support feedback-assisted pitch matching, but findings vary and pitch exercises do not establish whole-song mastery. VoiceRemedy has not had a human learning-outcomes trial.

84 tests pass. Source builds and Windows/Android packaging were checked. Tests remained silent with synthetic microphone input; physical-device performance and subjective listening still need review.
